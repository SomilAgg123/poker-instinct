Meet me at the river.

Build three five-card poker hands from a shared market. Beat the rival across the rivers and chase bonus contracts.

  1. Select one of the four market cards, then tap Place here on a river. Once placed, a card stays there.
  2. The rival takes one of the remaining cards. The other two are discarded. A fresh market appears.
  3. Repeat for 15 picks. Each river holds five cards. Twice per game, you may refresh all four market cards before choosing.
  4. Compare matching rivers. A win earns 3 points; a tie earns 1 each. Each fulfilled contract earns 2 points, even if that hand loses. Most total points wins.

Contracts

Ember: at least four hearts or diamonds combined.
Current: a straight or a straight flush.
Anchor: three of a kind, a full house, or four of a kind.

Poker ranking, highest first

Straight flush → four of a kind → full house → flush → straight → three of a kind → two pair → one pair → high card.

Standard kickers break ties. Aces are high, except in A-2-3-4-5. Suits never break ties. Hands are ranked only when complete; the labels before then describe your cards so far.

Make it tactical

Watch the rival's open hands. Denying a key card can be worth more than improving yours. Save a refresh for a tight finish. You act first on every market; the rival gets a reply, not a hidden advantage.

Cards already placed stay out of the deck. When fewer than four cards remain, all unplaced cards (including earlier discards) are reshuffled. The same card can return to the market, but never appear twice on the table. No jokers.
